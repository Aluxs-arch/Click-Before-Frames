# Support / Dukungan

**EN:** Found a bug, a translation mistake, or a physics issue? Open an issue
(in English or Bahasa Indonesia) at
[github.com/Aluxs-arch/Click-Before-Frames/issues](https://github.com/Aluxs-arch/Click-Before-Frames/issues).
Please include your Geode version, GD version, platform, FPS, and the values of
your *Before Frame* settings. Pull requests for new translations and fixes are
welcome. Developer: **Aluxs** — GitHub **@Aluxs-arch**.
